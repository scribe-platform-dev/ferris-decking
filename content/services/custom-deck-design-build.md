We start with a conversation, not a catalog. Grade, drainage, sun path, how you
entertain — it all goes into a layout before a single board is cut. Framing is
pressure-treated or steel on request, fasteners are hidden where the design
calls for it, and every build is pulled to the township's permit before
footings go in the ground.
