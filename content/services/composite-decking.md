Capped composite boards hold their color through Lancaster County's full swing
of seasons — humid summers, freeze-thaw winters — without the splintering or
graying that reclaims an untreated deck in a few years. We carry a handful of
board families in a range of wood-tone colors and will bring physical samples
to your house before you decide on anything.
