Every restoration starts with us underneath the deck, not on top of it —
checking ledger attachment, joist hangers, and post footings before we talk
about how the surface looks. Where the structure is sound, that means
board replacement, railing repair, stripping, and a fresh stain or sealant.
Where it isn't, we'll say so plainly rather than resurface over a problem.
